Who I Am

ABOUT Me

I’m a creative professional working across Video Editing, Color Grading, Cinematography, VFX Compositing, and Creative Direction. Over the years, I’ve built experience in both the technical and creative sides of visual storytelling, working on projects ranging from films and advertisements to branded and digital content.

My journey started with production and on-set coordination, where I worked as a Production Coordinator with Visual Bird Studio. During this time, I collaborated with a team on multiple South Indian film projects, gaining strong experience in production workflow, team coordination, and large-scale project execution.

After that, I worked as a Video Editor and Videographer at Global Skills Park for nearly a year, where I handled content production, shooting, editing, and visual storytelling for various media requirements.

Currently, I work with Advertising 360 as a Video Editor and Videographer, while also contributing as a Colorist and Creative Director during shoots. I actively participate in planning visuals, directing scenes, managing cinematic compositions, and ensuring the final output matches the creative vision from shoot to post-production.

My work focuses on creating visually strong and cinematic content with attention to storytelling, color, composition, and detail. Along with editing and cinematography, I also work on VFX compositing, motion graphics, and 3D-related creative workflows.
